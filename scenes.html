Visual Storyboard · Episode 1 in Production

The Run to Tincup

From The Big Shop in Park County, past the steaming mineral pools of Mount Princeton, to staging in the ghost town of St. Elmo at 10,000 feet. Every locked keyframe in strict 2D construction-paper cutout collage animation.

Cold Open · Shot 0.1 County Road · Grant · 8,500 ft

The Dawn Convertible Run

Fat paper snowflakes tumble over a frozen mountain highway as Ridge Glider's 2014 muscle-car convertible rumbles through with the top stubbornly down. Ridge drives one-handed sipping steaming coffee while Angel the living laptop is cinched tightly into the passenger bucket seat with the shoulder sash.

Angel: "That's not a flake, Ridge. That's a SNOWflake."
Ridge Glider: "Top stays down."
Scene 01 · Shot 1.1 Park County · 8,500 ft

The Big Shop Exterior

Heavy paper snowflakes blanket the yard at Anvill Customs. Anvill's lifted sand-tan 4x4 pickup truck idles beside his tall custom enclosed trailer, while Ridge Glider's 2014 muscle-car convertible sits stubbornly top-down in the freezing snow.

Ridge Glider: "If the sky is open, the top is down. Air conditioning is free in Colorado."
Scene 01 · Shot 1.2 Anvill Customs Bay

The Master Interior

Anvill locks down his custom black-and-red mountain sled in the shop bay with yellow ratchet straps. Ridge Glider sips boiling coffee in his helmet and peak-reflection goggles, while Angel monitors telemetry from the steel workbench in her miniature dial-lace boots.

Angel: "Current telemetry on Tincup Pass: fourteen below, sustained winds forty-two knots, and the avalanche advisory is simply: 'DON'T.'"
Scene 01 · Shot 1.5 Anvill Customs Bay

Trail Boss Takes the Bench

High-stakes ER trauma nurse and mountain marshal Trail Boss slams her heavy medical go-bag onto the bench with an affectionate, radiant grin—ready with trauma shears the second Ridge Glider's bravado defies gravity.

Trail Boss: "Ridge, I just clocked twelve hours in the ER stitching weekend warriors together. I am NOT splinting your femur at twelve thousand feet in a whiteout."
Scene 01 · Shot 1.6 Anvill Customs Bay

The Blue Smoke Entrance

Billows of opaque pale-blue two-stroke exhaust roll through the roll-up bay door. Tall Hillbilly coughs violently in comic lot-rent panic clutching his wrench and baling wire, while compact Woody strolls in deadpan-carrying Hillbilly's green snowmobile hood under his arm like a folded newspaper.

Woody: "Told ya, cuz. But Ridge said 'blower powder' and Ridge bought the two-stroke oil... Hey Ridge, you need a cord of wood?"
Scene 01 · Shot 1.7 Anvill Customs Bay Door

Medusa & The SledRats Arrival

Medusa roars up to the bay door on her custom teal-and-pink mountain sled, stepping off with her signature serpentine braids whipping and cat-eye goggles pushed up with a competitive mountain smirk to challenge Ridge Glider to the Continental Divide.

Medusa: "SledRats already drew straws for who gets to tow Ridge Glider when he buries his front bumper. Just like Georgia Pass when you tried to shake us on a super jump."
Scene 01 · Shot 1.8 The Big Shop

Mama Hen's Entrance

The glamorous cabin matriarch enters from the back office in her luxury burgundy faux-fur puffer jacket and gold hoops, setting down a 4-gallon commercial stainless coffee urn with total authority while affectionately plucking a stray snowflake off Ridge Glider's collar.

Mama Hen: "Sixteen years since that little lounge in Iowa, Ridge Glider, and you still think hot air and mountain swagger keep you warm. Drink your coffee before I drag you back to the cabin."
Scene 02 · Shot 2.1 Chalk Creek · CR 162 · 8,250 ft

Chalk Creek & The Hot Springs

The truck and trailer convoy rolls west beneath the sheer white Chalk Cliffs. Plumes of mineral steam billow into the sub-zero air as tourists in plush terrycloth bathrobes hold wine glasses, staring open-mouthed at the rumbling snowmobile armada.

Hillbilly (over CB): "Hey Ridge! Them hot springs look mighty warm! Sure we can't take a quick dip?!"
Ridge Glider: "Earn the soak first, Hillbilly! St. Elmo is ahead!"
Scene 02 · Shot 2.2 Mount Princeton Resort Lobby · 8,250 ft

Mama Hen's Spa Check-In

While the crew rolls on to freeze in sub-zero snow on the Divide, Mama Hen checks into the luxury Mount Princeton resort lodge. Beaming in her burgundy metallic fur-trimmed puffer, she slides Ridge Glider's card across the log counter, sipping a tropical cocktail while steam billows from the mineral pools outside.

Mama Hen: "Put the penthouse suite on Ridge Glider's card, honey. The boys are going up on Tincup Pass to freeze their tailpipes off, but Momma’s having a spa day!"
Scene 03 · Shot 3.1 St. Elmo Ghost Town · 10,000 ft

St. Elmo Ghost Town Staging

The plowed road ends dead in an 8-foot snowbank in front of 1880s false-front mining storefronts. Anvill drops the heavy aluminum trailer ramp into the snowpack, the fleet of mountain sleds idles in the powder with Angel mounted to Ridge's dash, and Droney buzzes overhead to film the 4K launch.

Angel: "Telemetry synced. Droney deployed. If you yard-sale on camera today, Daddy, at least your subscribers will get sixty frames per second in 4K."
Scene 03 · Shot 3.2 St. Elmo Trailer Ramp · 10,000 ft

The Trailhead Roll Call

Trail Boss stands poised in the deep powder at the trailer ramp with her radio conducting trail roll call. Ridge Glider clicks Angel into her custom heated dash bracket on his orange mountain sled as Droney hovers overhead capturing 4K telemetry.

Trail Boss (into radio): "Alright, roll call! Sleds checked? Beacons on send? Radios on channel four? We do not ride until everyone is accounted for!"
Angel (winking on screen): "Telemetry synced, Boss. If Daddy gets stuck in a tree well again, I've already geotagged the rescue chopper."
Scene 04 · Shot 4.0 Tincup Pass Cornice · 11,500 ft

Ridge Glider's Big Air Jump

Above tree line, Ridge Glider pins the throttle off an eight-foot wind cornice, launching his orange mountain sled sixty feet into the crystal alpine sky. Suspended weightless above the Continental Divide, he pulls a classic 90s heel-clicker trick while Droney catches 4K slow-motion underneath.

Angel (HUD telemetry flashing): "ALTITUDE: 52 FEET. FLIGHT PATH: CERTIFIED STUPID. DADDY STATUS: ABSOLUTELY SENDING IT!"
Scene 04 · Shot 4.1 Tincup Pass Drift · 11,500 ft

Hillbilly's Lawn-Dart & Woody's Catch

Hillbilly attempts a jump on his 1989 clunker, hits a hidden stump, and launches headfirst into a 12-foot snowdrift with his boots kicking frantically in the air. The clunker's ski snaps off, and Woody casually catches the flying ski out of mid-air with one hand while sipping coffee.

Woody (sipping coffee, holding runaway ski): "Told ya that cotter pin was made of baling wire, cuz."
Hillbilly (muffled from deep inside snowdrift): "Mmph! Durn pine tree jumped right in front of me!"
Scene 04 · Shot 4.2 Tincup Pass Trench · 11,500 ft

Woody's Monster Deadlift

Hillbilly's clunker shreds a drive belt in waist-deep powder. Woody calmly steps into the snow trench and bare-hand deadlifts the entire 450-pound snowmobile waist-high with raw timber-bucking strength, holding it rock-steady while Anvill drops to his knees swapping the belt NASCAR-pit-crew style.

Woody (holding 450-lb sled waist-high): "Hurry up, Anvill. Arms are gettin' cold. Still got that cord of seasoned lodgepole if you change your mind, Ridge."
Hillbilly (holding baling wire): "Told ya Woody was good for somethin'! Best wood hauler in Park County!"
Scene 04 · Shot 4.3 Continental Divide Headwall · 12,000 ft

The Divide Sidehill Race

Ridge Glider and Medusa duel neck-and-neck across a steep 45-degree powder headwall beneath the Continental Divide. Both riders sidehill on one ski, counter-steering along the slope and throwing twin towering rooster tails of fresh champagne powder.

Medusa (over engine braap): "Tie race, Ridge! First round at the hot springs bar is on both of us!"
Angel: "Tie, my foot. Angel was driving."
Scene 04 · Shot 4.4 Continental Divide Crest · 12,154 ft

The Tincup Pass Summit Marker

Ridge Glider and Medusa crest the high alpine cornice side-by-side, cutting their throttles at the weathered wooden summit marker: TINCUP PASS — ELEV. 12,154 FT. The sweeping Taylor Park basin opens below them under a crisp high-altitude sky.

Medusa: "Tie race, Ridge! First round at the hot springs bar is on both of us!"
Angel: "For the record, I requested braking four times. He declined all four."
Ridge Glider: "She navigates, I ignore her. Deal! Now look down there!"
Scene 05 · Shot 5.1 Tincup Main Street · 10,157 ft

Arrival in Snowbound Tincup

The convoy conquers the brutal 12,154 ft Divide and arrives in the silent ghost town of Tincup, completely buried in 10 feet of snowpack. Trail Boss conducts the final roll call beside her pink-and-black sled while Anvill in his black-and-red riding gear leans against his mountain sled.

Trail Boss (into radio): "Final roll call: Every single rider accounted for. Nobody left on the Divide."
Anvill: "Told ya my belt setup would hold, Boss. Now where's the hot coffee?"
Scene 06 · Finale Mount Princeton Hot Springs · Night

The Reward at Mount Princeton

Under a starry Colorado alpine sky and the softly lit Chalk Cliffs, the crew soaks in the steaming mineral pools. Ridge Glider and Mama Hen laugh over drinks against the river rocks while Angel relaxes dry under a tiny cabana umbrella on the poolside bar table.

Angel (amber telemetry winking): "Battery recharge at 98%. Core temperature nominal. Daddy survived the run to Tincup with only minor contusions."
Ridge Glider: "Top stays down, baby."
Angel: "Top stays down, Daddy." [FADE OUT]
